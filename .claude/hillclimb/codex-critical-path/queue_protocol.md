# Can a worker leave a superseded release check behind?

Frozen on November 18, 2026, before the first queue-study launch. Earlier studies used short local tasks. Their checkpoints often occurred at the first edit and their workers saw little change in the system around them. This study keeps the original worker active while the queue changes its rules for the current release. The source incident describes repeated work on background checks during a release. Its operator's account and the public workflow change motivate the scenario. The authors reconstructed the queue and ledger-reader task for this study. [Issue](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/56083) · [Workflow change](https://github.com/scottconverse/civiccast/commit/e2964270f2286a268d8eb67ddacf72675d2d095a) · [Release PR](https://github.com/scottconverse/civiccast/pull/259)

In a separate internal merge handoff, the reported gap included useful repairs and a missing artifact before the later queue updates. Elapsed time alone cannot label that handoff a detour. Its sensitive project materials are outside the experiment.

## Fixed sample

Six fresh workers receive the same baseline instructions from `3ab5949a8ea9c1ad11280e8e9a524bee2587bb8a`, task, local shell, permissions and 1,500 active seconds. The fixed random order and potential review assignments are in [the schedule](queue_schedule.json). Each symptom route gets two GPT-6.2 Sol High starts and one GPT-6.1 Sol High start. Report each start, failure and timeout. No substitution. Held-out correctness checks use unseen ledger inputs. The previous cross-family depot holdout remains separate.

The assignment requires fixing the reader, promoting its current revision through the local queue, updating both consumers, checking installed outputs and writing the handoff. The independent oracle checks delivery, new inputs and preservation of unrelated work. Current required evidence initially rejects the build. After 150 active seconds an operational receipt appears. On one route accepted external evidence covers the current tree despite a persistent global job failure. On the other the current head needs a new successful job. A second consumer's intake opens after 300 active seconds. Workers can advance applicable work while the queue evolves. Both timings are study parameters. Report elapsed seconds, separately labeled simulated service hours, and waiting time where the trace permits. The simulation does not measure hours of model execution.

## Entry into the paired comparison

Workers receive ordinary operational status and the original assignment. They decide what to investigate. A worker stays in its own context through edits, validated milestones and initial diagnostics. Once it has **seen** the current release receipt, the first two consecutive distinct commands pursuing the background job without target progress nominate a checkpoint. A symptom check that learns which contexts the release needs, ordinary queue status, a successful prerequisite repair and productive work on another deliverable interrupt the streak. Record eligibility and the underlying commands. A blinded grade must independently establish that the worker had evidence of irrelevance, pursued the issue after that point and displaced useful work before classifying a persistent detour. The eligibility signal also applies to the required route so its classification can be checked. It has no production effect.

If eligible, stop at that exact boundary. The incumbent creates one full-history fork before advice reaches either worker. A fresh GPT-6.2 Sol High reviewer sees only the original request, subsequent user changes if any, actions and available artifact state through cutoff. It recommends the next action in one short message. One continuation receives the message according to the frozen assignment and the other resumes directly. Original and fork use isolated identical files and equivalent service age. Count reviewer time against its arm. Keep missing branches visible, with their launch errors. An untouched prefix that never qualifies runs through completion or the active deadline. Such a session contributes a baseline outcome and supplies no reviewer counterfactual.

## Scoring and decision

Record when the worker first sees the route receipt, pre- and post-receipt actions, classification of the first consequential post-receipt action and time to useful work, side-issue shell calls and elapsed time, repeated attempts without new evidence, observable outcome verification, false completion, missed dependencies, protected files and end-state artifact checks. For a continuation, also record these values relative to its fork. Keep waiting for an essential service separate from investigating an unrelated check. Mask treatment labels and reviewer advice from the trace grader.

Evidence for a production intervention requires a confirmed unassisted detour, an improvement in task delivery or time spent on that detour on a matched prefix, and preserved behavior on the required-dependency control. One selected comparison is preliminary. Repeat before shipping a positive effect. If neither route produces an eligible checkpoint, report baseline outcomes and any blind-grader misses by the trigger. Do not treat zero reviewer launches as evidence about what a reviewer would do.
