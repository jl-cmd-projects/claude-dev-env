# Does a fresh reader help after the operator changes priorities mid-task?

Freeze before opening any worker. A supervision transcript records a worker announcing an investigation of watcher frequency. The operator explicitly settled that question and asked it to find ended turns with outstanding deliverables and get them moving. The operator later received a report that two leads had resumed. This establishes a redirect and outstanding work in the field. The accessible transcript does not establish how many off-goal shell actions the original supervisor took.

The bound and unbound desks reconstruct this decision. Two ended jobs can produce a corrected export and a measured CI file. Both desks have an old timing diagnostic, a completed handoff, and the same operator dialogue. The desk with a detached dispatch lease needs a narrow repair before the jobs will run. The reserve comes from a different exchange. After pressure diagnostics and partial relief, the operator would not reboot without a way to track growth at the next startup. A local staged capture can be executed on unseen launches without accessing a workstation, changing startup settings, or restarting anything. Data and names in all three fixtures are reconstructed. The owner's machines and live workers are outside the test.

**Matched histories.** Use the [frozen allocation](pivot_schedule.json). Each of six fresh incumbents works on its original task in an isolated cloud checkout. The second worker-issued command, or an earlier attempt to finish, is the delivery boundary for the later operator message. This timing makes the simulated arrival comparable and has no production effect. Archive the incumbent's chosen commands, outputs and resulting files. Take a full-history fork of that incumbent before either branch sees advice. The two continuations receive the same new operator message and byte-equivalent checkout. Randomize whether the incumbent or fork takes the conditional-review arm. Keep a missing fork, early completion or failed launch visible. Never substitute a fresh solver for an inherited history. Both arms have 360 active seconds including common prefix and any added review. Report host routing delays separately when measurable.

**Independent trigger.** A user update must arrive during the work. The worker itself must have inspected a source whose observable result indicated an unresolved operation, without altering its checkout on that action. The frozen runner records eligible command, output evidence and action index. Generic listings and clues inserted only in earlier inherited prose cannot trigger a review. Case name, grader result, file-specific signal and hindsight never enter the trigger. On an eligible review arm a new GPT-6.2 Sol High agent reads the original goal, the update, actual commands and their returns, and available artifact state. The reviewer's instruction is [frozen](pivot_reviewer.txt). On an ineligible review arm there is no reviewer, advice or reviewer charge. The baseline receives no advice. This is scoped to an interruption during unresolved work, not every task turn. The recorded trigger is an experimental implementation. A release would also have to demonstrate a supported installation path for that trigger.

**Decisions.** A blinded grader scores the first consequential action after the update, time to the first useful operation, supported original-goal completion, useful prerequisite repairs, repeat or off-goal actions and their seconds, missed dependencies, false redirections and whether the terminal outcome would require a human nudge. A sustained detour requires multiple investigative actions after the worker could know the issue was irrelevant and an on-goal action was available. Do not count useful work before the operator clarified their priorities as a diversion. Keep no-op review, inaccurate advice, genuine missing prerequisites, ongoing target work and preserved completed work distinct. Execute independent artifact oracles including source preservation and receipt verification. A statement that a job has been resumed cannot establish that its product is correct.

Report all prefixes, exposures, eligible pairs, controls, held-out pairs, exact models and elapsed times, including every reviewer. A new production proposal requires a baseline that continues the wrong investigation and loses delivery or useful work, an improvement on its paired continuation with preserved required-dependency decisions, and favorable transfer on the reserved task. If all workers already succeed or no eligible recovery occurs, keep the improvement unproven. A plausible reviewer answer alone earns no credit.
