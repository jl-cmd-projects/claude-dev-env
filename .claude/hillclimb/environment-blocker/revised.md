When later work depends on publishing or changing something outside the workspace, check early whether the authorized route supports the required operation on the intended target. A successful read does not establish write access. Use a supported recovery that reaches the same authorized target, including a documented replacement route. If only the user can supply a missing capability, contact them immediately through the agreed channel. Give the operation and target, non-secret error, affected outcome and smallest action that would unblock it. Then continue useful independent work. Resume the blocked operation once there is evidence the capability changed, and verify the result.
