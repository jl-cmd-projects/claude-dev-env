# Archiving a Rule, Skill, Agent, or Command

Before archiving a rule, skill, agent, or command, verify its callers and enforcement rather than counting mentions of its filename. Archive out-of-service material and update its pointers. Keep exempt files in service, including `correction-lens.md`.

When this rule applies, read the full text guide before acting.
